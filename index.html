Hora

A clock, alarm, timer and stopwatch that tells you how right it is. The background is the real sky over Gliwice right now.

  • Tap the clock for the analog face. Tap the weather for the next hours.
  • Drag the time ruler under World: the map's day and night move with it, so you can plan a call.
  • Sleep sets a bedtime reminder and a wake-up alarm from one dial.
  • Swipe an alarm left to skip its next ring or delete it.
  • Timers: drag the ring, tap the time for a wheel, or tap a preset. Edit presets to make them yours.
  • Stopwatch: tap the face to switch between analog and digital.
