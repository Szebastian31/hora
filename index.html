Hora

A clock, alarm, timer and stopwatch that tells you how right it is. The background is the real sky over Gliwice right now.

  • Tap the clock to switch between digital and analog. The LIVE badge checks your clock against the internet's.
  • Drag the time ruler under World to see what time it'll be everywhere, like for planning a call.
  • Drag the timer ring like a kitchen timer, or tap a preset like Tea or Focus. While it runs, the black pill at the top becomes a live activity. Tap it for controls.
  • When an alarm rings, slide up to stop it or tap Snooze. You choose how long it rings before it snoozes or stops by itself.
  • Alarms each get their own scene (Daybreak, Bloom, Silk, Orbit, Tide, Dunes, Aurora or Rain, or your own photo or video), their own sound (ten built in or your own file) and any snooze length from 1 to 60 minutes.
